## Sprechtext (EN, ca. 185 Wörter)

> You are looking at AncientVoid, a local-first hub for coding agents. In the next three minutes you will pair your phone and start your first monitored session. Run setup dot sh on your machine. It installs the hub, binds it to your tailnet, and prints a device token. Open the hub address on your phone, paste the token once, and the devices are paired. Back on the desktop, press the plus button. Pick the project, pick the adapter, type what you want done, and press Starten. The session appears instantly on both screens. On the phone you now see the live turn: the agent's steps stream in, folded into one turn card, with a status line that only claims what was measured. If the hub restarts, the session survives; state is rebuilt from the event log. One honest limitation: AncientVoid speaks to agents through adapters, so a brand-new CLI needs an adapter first. Everything you saw runs on your hardware; nothing leaves your tailnet. The repo link and the full guide with screenshots are on the endcard. Give it a star if this looks useful.
