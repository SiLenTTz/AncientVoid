## Sprechtext (EN, ca. 190 Wörter)

> This is the AncientVoid overview, the screen you will see most. Each card is one coding agent session: the project, the latest activity, and whether it waits for you, works, or rests. Cards that need you float to the top, so nothing rots in a background tab. Tabs switch the whole page between sessions, projects, hosts, and discovered sessions. Tap a card and the session opens: the chat is organized in turns, every turn folds its steps, and expanding one shows exactly what the agent did, including tool calls and their outputs. Scroll to the end after a reload: AncientVoid opens at the newest events, not at the beginning, so a long history never blocks you. The status line shows only measured facts; anything unknown says so and moves to the report tab. On the PC the same page fills a wide screen; on the phone it is one column, thumb-reachable. Watch several sessions at once from the PC workspace; we cover that in its own clip. The overview is honest by design: if something breaks, the card says what and when. Repo and guide links are on the endcard.
