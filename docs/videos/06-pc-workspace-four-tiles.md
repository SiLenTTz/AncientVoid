## Sprechtext (EN, ca. 183 Wörter)

> The PC workspace is for the days you babysit several agents at once. Open the PC page and sessions become tiles: one big, two side by side, one plus two, or a two by two grid. Switch layouts with the keyboard, the way a tiling window manager does; the layout you chose is remembered per machine. Each tile is a live session view, not a screenshot: turns stream, status lines update, and you can steer from the focused tile while the others keep rendering at a reduced rate, so four sessions do not melt your frame budget. Pop any view out into its own window when it deserves a whole screen; the graph, the file tree, and notes can be windows too. Keyboard-first is the design rule: open sessions, switch tiles, and drive the composer without touching the mouse. When a tile needs you, its frame says so; the overview remains the single place where nothing can hide. The workspace is a view, not a second hub: close the browser, and the sessions keep running. Repo and guide links are on the endcard.
